Baby, thank you for being so sweet to me. Even when you're sleepy and tired, you still remind me to take care of myself, eat my meals, and have enough energy for school. Those little things might seem simple to you, but they mean so much to me. They make me feel cared for in a way I can't fully explain.

I pray that He protects you wherever you go, gives you wisdom in everything you do, and fills your heart with peace and joy. Have a beautiful and blessed day, baby Julie. I love you today, tomorrow, and every day I get the privilege of choosing you. Mwaaaaa! 💋💚

With all the love in my heart, Your baby, always ♡
